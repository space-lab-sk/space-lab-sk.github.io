Education

Teaching, talks, and student research

Lectures at universities

Regular courses and guest lectures we contribute to at partner universities.

SIREN / Detection and Dosimetry of Cosmic Rays

A course on the detection and dosimetry of cosmic radiation, covering instrumentation principles and measurement techniques.

Ultra High Energy Particles

An introduction to the physics of the highest-energy particles observed in nature and the experiments built to detect them.

Plasma in Space

Fundamentals of space plasma physics — the solar wind, magnetospheres, and plasma processes throughout the Solar System.

SK-S2P_Edu

An educational programme on space science and space physics developed for Slovak university students.

Public education

Regular public-facing formats that bring our research to a general audience.

SPACE::TALK

A monthly meetup on space research and engineering, held on the first Thursday of the month at SPACE::LAB, Bulharská 2. Interactive talks by leading Slovak experts, with time for networking.

Most recent: #36 Planetárna obrana: vieme dnes obrániť Zem? — Peter Vereš, 11.6.2026

SPACE::LAB Summer School

An annual summer school for students, most recently "From Space Data to Predictions" — hands-on work with real space-weather and cosmic-ray datasets under the guidance of our researchers.

2024 edition: Od kozmických dát k predikciám
