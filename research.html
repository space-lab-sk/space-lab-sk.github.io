Space Science

Four questions we ask about space

Cosmic Rays Science

How does the Sun shape the flux of galactic cosmic rays reaching Earth?

We track solar modulation, Forbush decreases and ground-level enhancements in the uninterrupted neutron monitor record from Lomnický štít, and model how charged particles propagate through the heliosphere and magnetosphere.

Read more →
Neutrino Science

Where do the Universe's highest-energy neutrinos come from?

Through the KM3NeT deep-sea telescope we search for the cosmic accelerators behind astrophysical neutrinos — messengers that arrive undeflected by magnetic fields and unabsorbed by matter.

Read more →
Space Weather Science

Can we predict when solar activity will disrupt technology on Earth and in orbit?

We study how coronal mass ejections, the solar wind and geomagnetic storms couple to spacecraft, aviation and ground infrastructure — and apply machine learning to forecast their impact.

Read more →
Airglow Science

What does the faint glow of the upper atmosphere reveal about the boundary to space?

By measuring airglow emission we probe the dynamics, waves and composition of the region where Earth's atmosphere meets space — and characterise the background that limits observations from orbit.

Read more →

Space Physics Laboratory · Lomnický štít

One of the longest cosmic-ray records on Earth

A laboratory at 2634 metres

High in the Tatra mountains, our laboratory has recorded the secondary particles produced when cosmic rays strike the atmosphere without interruption for decades. Thin air and a stable mountain platform make it one of Europe's most valuable observing sites.

Services & Products

What we offer to partners

Cleanroom Facilities

The first dedicated space cleanroom in Slovakia — ISO 7, with ESD protection and controlled temperature and humidity, for developing, testing and integrating spaceborne hardware to ECSS standards.

Facility details →

OpenLab Capabilities

As part of the OPENLABS SAV project we are building a dedicated open-access space at Lomnický štít where partner institutions and companies can install and operate their own instruments. We provide the site, infrastructure and operational support — power, connectivity, environmental monitoring and on-site logistics at 2634 m.
